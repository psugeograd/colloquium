Rules and Judging Guidelines¶

Judging Guidelines¶

  • Download the Judging Rubric

Attendance¶

  • All students are strongly encouraged to participate in this department event (no matter their year). Giving a talk or poster will help you improve your communication skills and there are opportunities to win prizes.

Oral Presentations¶

  • Talks will take place both on Thursday the 16th and Friday the 17th of April (morning and afternoon).
  • Talks will be presented live and will be 12 minutes long with 3 minutes of questions and will be timed. Any talk exceeding 15 minutes will be interrupted.
  • THe presentation file should be submitted by Apr 15th

Poster Presentations¶

  • Poster presentations will be in person only.
  • The maximum poster size is 42 × 60 inches. Orientation does not matter.
  • Make sure all features on the posters are clearly visible (figures, font sizes)
  • Posters should be printed by Apr 14th. The presenters need to send their poster file to Laura Rush (lbr5310@psu.edu) to print it out.
