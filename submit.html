Photo Contest¶

Show off your lab and fieldwork photos! Submit pictures from your microscope, in the field, or of your work. Be sure to include the location. Photos should be from the past year (summer 2025–now).

The department favorite will be used as the Graduate Symposium abstract booklet cover.

Submit your photos here

Deadline: March 6, 2026 at 5:00 pm

A voting form will be sent out on March 17. Voting closes April 3.
